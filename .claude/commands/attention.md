description: Everything missed, due this week, unchecked, lapsing or quiet, in one list.
---

Run `npm run docket -- attention`.

Present it in this order, one line each:
1. **Missed.** Statutory deadlines past their date. Say plainly that the right may already be lost, and that the attorney decides today whether an extension, a restoration or a hearing request is open. Never suggest back-dating anything.
2. **Due this week.** With the attorney and whether a second person has checked the date.
3. **Renewals.** Lapsed, in grace (late fees running), and anything due in 45 days without instructions.
4. **Nothing docketed.** Live cases with no next date, and registered rights with no renewal on file.
5. **Quiet.** Pending cases with nothing on file for 120 days.

End with the three things to do first, each naming a person.
